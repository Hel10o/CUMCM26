Welcome to join the challenging contest - CUMCM (Contemporary Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling)

CUMCM-2026 will start at 6:00PM, 10 Sept. 2026 and end at 8:00PM, 13 Sept. 2026. (Beijing Time)
Please register before 6:00PM 09 Sept. 2026 (Beijing Time).
For participants from higher education institutions outside the Chinese Mainland, Hong Kong SAR, Macao SAR and Taiwan, China, the registration fee will be waived.
